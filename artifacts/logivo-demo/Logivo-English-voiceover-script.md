# Logivo — English voice-over script

Target length: **3:00**. Read in a confident, clear, friendly voice at about **125–130 words per minute**. Leave short natural pauses between sections. The final video is intentionally silent.

## 00:00–00:08 — Introduction

Meet Logivo, the connected freight platform that makes every delivery clearer, faster, and easier to manage.

## 00:08–00:20 — Client home screen

The client starts from one simple dashboard. Active orders, current status, recent activity, and the most important actions are immediately visible.

## 00:20–00:35 — Client overview and actions

Performance cards show spending, completed orders, and recent trends. Quick actions make it easy to create a listing, search existing listings, review orders, open analytics, or continue a conversation.

## 00:35–00:50 — Create a freight listing

Creating a shipment is structured and straightforward. The client adds cargo details, weight, volume, handling requirements, vehicle type, route, delivery window, and a proposed price in the preferred currency.

## 00:50–01:04 — Real-time tracking

Once a trip is active, the client can follow it on the map. Route progress, stops, timing, driver position, and important alerts stay together in one live tracking view.

## 01:04–01:16 — Client chat

Built-in chat keeps communication linked to the order. The client and driver can share updates, confirm details, and resolve questions without leaving Logivo.

## 01:16–01:28 — Client balances

Order funds and the Logivo commission are separated by purpose. The same balances can be viewed and funded in Uzbek soʻm or US dollars, with reserved amounts clearly shown.

## 01:28–01:34 — Driver transition

Now, let’s switch to the driver experience: find the right load, match, and deliver.

## 01:34–01:46 — Driver home screen

The driver dashboard shows availability, current city, return-load options, earnings, active orders, and personalized matches based on the vehicle and regular lanes.

## 01:46–02:02 — Search for loads

Drivers can search and filter available shipments by location, date, price, and trust indicators. Each card highlights the route, cargo weight, client rating, proposed price, and vehicle compatibility.

## 02:02–02:18 — Load details and bidding

Before accepting, the driver reviews the client’s trust score, cargo information, route, market-price insight, and estimated trip profit. A suitable load can be accepted immediately, or the driver can submit a bid.

## 02:18–02:32 — Active delivery workflow

Active orders use a clear step-by-step workflow from client confirmation through pickup and delivery. The next required action is always visible, while tracking keeps the trip connected in real time.

## 02:32–02:46 — Driver profile and documents

The driver profile brings together identity, rating, trust score, vehicles, regular lanes, bids, order history, documents, reviews, statistics, saved searches, and tracking tools.

## 02:46–02:56 — Driver commission balance

Drivers use one commission balance. It can be displayed or topped up in soʻm or dollars, while the commission per order remains transparent and configurable.

## 02:56–03:00 — Closing

Logivo. Move freight with confidence.
